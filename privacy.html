Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy ("Policy") explains how Yui, operator of the website yui-rui.yingternet.com (the "Site"), collects, uses, stores, and protects the information of visitors ("you"). Your privacy matters to us. We are committed to handling any personal information transparently and honestly.

1. Information We Collect

The Site is a personal link-in-bio static page that simply displays links to social media profiles. During normal browsing, the Site itself does not ask you to provide any personal information and does not set tracking cookies.

  • Nothing we ask for: We do not ask you to enter your name, email, phone number, or any other personally identifiable information.
  • Server logs: To keep the service secure and running properly, our web server (hosted by third-party platforms) may routinely log basic connection data, such as IP address, browser type, and visit time. This data is used solely for security and technical operations, is not used to identify individuals, and is not cross-referenced with other data sources.

2. How We Use Information

Any basic connection data that may be automatically obtained is used only for the following purposes:

  • Maintaining the security and proper functioning of the Site;
  • Diagnosing and fixing technical issues;
  • Preventing abuse, attacks, or unauthorized access;
  • Understanding overall traffic through anonymous statistics to improve the experience.

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We will not disclose your information to any third party except in the following circumstances:

  • Where legally required by law, regulation, or a government authority;
  • Where necessary to protect the rights or safety of the Site, our users, or the public;
  • Third-party infrastructure required for core services (such as web hosting and domain-name services), whose processing is limited to technical operations.

4. Data Storage and Security

The Site protects data transmission through an encrypted connection (HTTPS) between you and us. Because the Site is a static showcase page that stores almost no personal data, the risk of a data breach is relatively low. We still take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect any limited data we may hold.

5. Cookies

The Site itself does not place tracking cookies. If you follow links from the Site to third-party platforms (such as X, Bluesky, Instagram, Threads, or YouTube), their privacy policies apply separately, and we neither control nor accept responsibility for their data processing.

6. Children's Privacy

The Site's content is for general informational display. If you are under the legal age in your jurisdiction, please browse under the supervision of a guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Third-Party Links

The Site may contain links to third-party websites. Clicking such links takes you away from the Site, your information is subject to those third parties' own policies, and we are not responsible for their practices.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time to reflect legal, technical, or operational changes. Any changes will be posted on this page and take effect upon posting. If a change is significant, we will bring it to your attention in a prominent way.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle data, please reach out to us through the social channels shown on the Site.
